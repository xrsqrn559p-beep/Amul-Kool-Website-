Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Amul Kool is a delicious, refreshing sterilized flavoured milk beverage from the house of Amul. Made with high-quality fresh milk and premium natural extracts, it offers healthy nutrition and pure refreshment in every sip.
Yes, all Amul Kool flavoured milk products are 100% vegetarian. They contain only pure dairy milk, sugar, and natural/nature-identical flavouring ingredients.
Amul Kool bottles are sterilized and hermetically sealed, so they can be stored at room temperature before opening. However, we highly recommend chilling them before drinking to experience the ultimate refreshing taste. Once opened, keep refrigerated and consume within 24 hours.
Due to the advanced UHT sterilization processing and secure packaging, unopened Amul Kool bottles have a shelf life of up to 180 days from the date of manufacture. Please check the neck of the bottle for the exact best before date.
Amul Kool is available at all major grocery stores, supermarkets, local Amul parlors, and leading online delivery platforms. You can also order directly through the Buy Online section of this website or use our Store Locator to find a shop near you.
Amul Kool is available in a variety of delicious flavours including Rose, Kesar (Saffron), Elaichi (Cardamom), Badam (Almond), Chocolate, and Coffee.
